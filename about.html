The Person Behind the Code

< THE STORY />

I didn't start with code.

I've always been drawn to creating, learning, and figuring out how things work. Long before I started building websites, I was already interested in the ideas behind them—how something could look, feel, communicate, and become an experience for someone else.

Create Build Learn

Web development gave those instincts somewhere to meet. Design became something I could make interactive. Problem-solving became something I could build into a system. And every project became another opportunity to learn something I didn't know before.

I didn't choose between being creative and being technical.

I found a space where I could be both.

< HOW I GOT HERE />

The path wasn't exactly linear.

Melanin Coded grew from years of creating, experimenting, learning new tools, and following ideas far enough to see what they could become.

01

Curiosity

First came the need to figure things out.

I've always learned by exploring—asking questions, testing ideas, and paying attention to why something works instead of stopping at the fact that it does.

02

Creating

Ideas needed somewhere to become real.

Creating digital content and visual concepts taught me to think about identity, presentation, audience, and the feeling an experience leaves behind.

03

Building

Then I wanted to understand what made it work.

Learning web development turned that curiosity into something tangible. I could design an idea, write the code behind it, solve the problems in between, and watch the result become functional.

04

Melanin Coded

Eventually, the pieces became a direction.

Melanin Coded is where those pieces come together: creativity, development, experimentation, and the belief that something functional can still have a distinct point of view.

< WHAT I BRING />

My approach isn't just about what I can build. It's also about how I think while building it.

Thoughtful.
01 / Intention

I pay attention to the small decisions because they shape the larger experience—from hierarchy and spacing to what happens after someone clicks.

Curious.
02 / Exploration

I like understanding the why behind the work. If I don't know how to do something yet, my instinct is to research it, test it, and learn.

Functional.
03 / Purpose

A website can have personality and still do its job. I care about making the experience useful, understandable, responsive, and intentional.

Human.
04 / Perspective

Behind every screen is a person trying to find, understand, buy, book, learn, or connect. I try not to lose sight of that while building.

*

Pretty matters. Purpose matters too.

< MY APPROACH />

Good work starts before the code does.

I like to understand what we're building, who it's for, and what it needs to accomplish before deciding what it should look like.

01

Listen First

Understand the idea before designing the answer.

Every project has different goals, priorities, and people behind it. I want to understand those pieces first instead of forcing every project into the same solution.

02

Design With Reason

Make choices that earn their place.

Color, type, layout, movement, and interaction should support the experience—not compete with it. I like creative choices, but I want them to have a reason for being there.

03

Build Thoughtfully

Think beyond the first screen.

Responsive behavior, interactions, integrations, and the details behind the interface matter too. The goal is an experience that holds together beyond the initial impression.

04

Test & Refine

Keep looking until it feels right.

I test, adjust, question, and revisit the details. Sometimes the difference between something working and something feeling finished is one small change.

process ≠ formula

A consistent process doesn't require a repetitive result.
