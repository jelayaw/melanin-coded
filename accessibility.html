Our Commitment

Accessibility starts with intention.

Our Commitment

Melanin Coded is committed to creating digital experiences that are welcoming and usable for as many people as possible. Accessibility is considered throughout the design and development process, and I continue to improve the Melanin Coded website as accessibility needs, standards, and technology evolve.

02

Accessibility Practices

Thoughtful choices, built in.

Accessibility Practices

When designing and developing this website, Melanin Coded works to support accessibility through practices such as:

  • 01

    Using semantic HTML and meaningful page structure.

  • 02

    Supporting keyboard navigation for interactive elements.

  • 03

    Providing visible focus states for links, buttons, and form controls.

  • 04

    Using labels and accessibility attributes where appropriate.

  • 05

    Providing alternative text for meaningful images.

  • 06

    Considering color contrast, typography, spacing, and readability.

  • 07

    Creating responsive layouts that work across different screen sizes and devices.

  • 08

    Building forms and interactive components with accessibility in mind.

03

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility keeps evolving.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process rather than a one-time checklist. As Melanin Coded grows and new pages, features, and technologies are introduced, the website may continue to be reviewed and improved to provide a better experience for visitors with different needs and assistive technologies.

04

Third-Party Content & Services

Some experiences extend beyond our code.

Third-Party Content & Services

Some parts of the website may rely on third-party platforms, services, integrations, or external websites that Melanin Coded does not fully control. While accessibility is considered when choosing and implementing these services, their accessibility may be governed by the individual provider.

05

Accessibility Support

Something getting in the way?

Tell me what you're experiencing.

If you experience difficulty using the Melanin Coded website, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need information provided in another reasonable format, please reach out.

When contacting Melanin Coded, it can be helpful to include the page or feature you were trying to use and a brief description of the issue. I'll make a reasonable effort to understand the problem and provide assistance or an alternative way to access the information when possible.
