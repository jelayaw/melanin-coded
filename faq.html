01

First things first.

Getting Started

Melanin Coded creates custom, responsive websites for businesses, brands, creatives, and entrepreneurs. Projects can range from focused landing pages to multi-page business websites and more advanced builds with features or integrations based on your needs.

Yes. You don't need to have everything figured out before reaching out. Melanin Coded can work with new businesses building their first online presence as well as established brands looking to redesign, expand, or improve an existing website.

Start by completing the Start a Project inquiry. You'll provide information about your website, goals, features, budget, timeline, and business so I can get a clear picture of what you're looking for before we move forward.

No. An inquiry is the first step, not a booking or client agreement. I'll review your project details, scope, timeline, and my current availability before determining whether the project is a good fit. No project date is reserved simply by submitting an inquiry.

You'll receive a welcome from Melanin Coded with your next steps, project details, and information about accessing your client portal. From there, we'll complete onboarding and any remaining requirements before your project officially begins.

02

Let's talk numbers.

Pricing &
Payments

Pricing depends on the size, complexity, features, and requirements of your project. Landing pages currently start at $350. Larger or more advanced websites are quoted according to scope after your project inquiry has been reviewed.

Yes. A 25% deposit is required to secure your project and begin work. Your project is not considered booked until the required deposit and onboarding requirements have been completed.

For larger projects, milestone payments may be available so the total project cost can be divided across agreed stages of the build. Your payment schedule will be established before work begins.

Buy Now, Pay Later availability depends on the payment options supported at the time of checkout. When available, eligible clients may be able to select a supported installment option through the payment provider. If one isn't available, a Melanin Coded milestone payment schedule may be an alternative.

Yes. Your project includes a set number of revision rounds based on its scope. Additional revisions beyond the included amount are currently $10 each and must remain within the agreed project scope. Larger additions or new functionality may require a separate quote.

If work has already begun, the project deposit is non-refundable. Any additional amounts due will depend on the work completed and the terms of your project agreement. If Melanin Coded must cancel a project before work begins, any applicable payment collected for unperformed work will be refunded.

03

From idea to launch.

The Build
Process

Timelines depend on the project's size and complexity. You'll receive an estimated project timeline based on your specific scope rather than a one-size-fits-all turnaround time. Timelines are presented in days or weeks and begin once the required onboarding materials are received.

Landing pages generally include 1–2 revision rounds, while larger builds may include 3–4, depending on the agreed scope. Your included revisions will be clearly stated before the project begins.

Yes. For projects that include a design or mockup stage, you'll have an opportunity to review the visual direction before full development moves forward. This helps us resolve major design decisions before spending time building the finished website.

The approved mockup establishes the visual direction for your website, but it is not a pixel-for-pixel guarantee of the final build. Responsive behavior, real content, functionality, browser differences, accessibility, and technical requirements may require reasonable adjustments during development. The goal is to preserve the approved design direction while making sure the finished website actually works well across devices.

Client delays can move the project timeline. Short delays may shift delivery by approximately 1–2 days, depending on the stage of the project. If required information or feedback is not provided for more than 5–7 days without communication, the project may be cancelled and the deposit forfeited.

Yes. Approved clients will have access to the Melanin Coded client portal, where project progress, stages, and important updates can be viewed throughout the build.

Yes. You'll have an opportunity to review and test your website before launch. This is the time to identify issues and make sure the completed project meets the agreed scope before final handoff.

04

Built for what comes next.

Ownership &
After Launch

Melanin Coded specializes in custom web development rather than relying exclusively on website builders. However, the right platform depends on the project. Shopify and other platforms or integrations may be used when they make sense for the client's needs, particularly when ongoing store or content management is important.

Once the final balance has been paid, ownership of the completed project is transferred according to the terms of your project agreement. Third-party services, licensed assets, platforms, fonts, plugins, and other external tools remain subject to their own licenses and terms.

Yes. Clients should ultimately own and control their domain, hosting, and other essential accounts rather than having those assets permanently tied to the developer. If you don't already have them, I can guide you through what you'll need for your project.

Yes. Melanin Coded websites are built with responsive design in mind so the layout can adapt across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

Yes. Optional website-care services are available for clients who would rather have Melanin Coded handle certain ongoing updates after launch.

Website Care — $25/month includes approximately 1–2 small website updates per month. Website Plus — $75/month includes up to 2 hours of website work per month. Unused updates or time do not roll over into future months.

Small additional revisions during the active project may be purchased when appropriate. After launch, one-time updates or an ongoing maintenance plan may be a better fit. Significant changes, new pages, new features, or work outside the original scope may require a separate quote.
