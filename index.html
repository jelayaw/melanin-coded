< Good Questions />

Before We Build, You Might Be Wondering...

A few quick answers to questions you may have before starting your project.

Do you build every website from scratch? +

Custom development is at the heart of Melanin Coded. Each project is approached around the needs of the business rather than forcing every client into the same design or template.

Do you create websites using WordPress, Wix, Shopify, etc.? +

Melanin Coded is custom-first, not custom-only. I primarily focus on custom development, but platforms such as Shopify, WordPress, and other tools may be used or integrated when they make sense for the project's needs, existing systems, and long-term goals.

How long does a website take? +

Timelines vary based on the project's size, functionality, content, and revision needs. Before development begins, you'll receive a project-specific timeline based on the scope of your website.

Do I need to have everything ready before contacting you? +

No. You can reach out while your idea is still taking shape. We'll identify what you already have, what your project needs, and what should be prepared before the build moves forward.
