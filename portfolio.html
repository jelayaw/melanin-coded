Full-Stack Ecommerce Development

The Barkery House.

A responsive ecommerce experience built beyond the storefront, combining customer-facing design with server-side pricing, database-backed orders, secure payment processing, transactional emails, and post-purchase fulfillment workflows.

View Live Site ↗
Full-Stack · Ecommerce
01

The Goal

Build an ecommerce experience that goes beyond a storefront mockup.

The Barkery House was created to practice the complete customer journey from browsing and cart management through checkout, payment confirmation, order storage, and fulfillment.

02

Development

Connected front-end experiences to real backend systems.

The project combines a custom responsive frontend with a Node and Express backend, Supabase data storage, Stripe Checkout, verified payment webhooks, order lifecycle management, and transactional email workflows.
