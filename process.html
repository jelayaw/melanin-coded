Stage One

Start With the Idea

< LET'S TALK />

You don't need all the answers.

You can come to me with a detailed plan, a rough concept, or something that still needs figuring out. We start by talking through the idea and identifying what the website actually needs to accomplish.

01 / GOAL

“I know what I want people to do when they visit.”

02 / CONTENT

“I have photos and some copy... now what?”

03 / FUNCTION

“Can customers book, buy, or contact me here?”

04 / DIRECTION

“I know what I like. I just don't know what it's called.”

Who is it for? What should it do? What matters most?
What happens here

We talk through your goals, audience, content, functionality, visual direction, timeline, and priorities so I can understand the scope of the project before recommending a solution.

By the end of this stage

The idea doesn't have to be finished. It just needs a direction.
