< FLEXIBLE PAYMENTS />

Professional work. More flexible ways to pay.

Every business has a different budget and timeline. Melanin Coded offers flexible payment structures so qualified projects can move forward without requiring the full project balance upfront.

01

25% Project Deposit

Approved projects begin with a 25% deposit based on your final project quote. The remaining balance follows the payment schedule outlined in your project agreement.

02

Milestone Payments

Longer or more involved projects may be divided into scheduled payments connected to major project stages, making larger builds easier to manage.

03

Extended Payment Arrangements

Some projects may qualify for a customized milestone schedule with additional time between payments. Extended payment arrangements also extend the project timeline and are approved case by case.

04

Eligible Pay-Over-Time Options

Eligible third-party payment options may be available at checkout. Availability, approval, spending limits, and repayment terms are determined by the payment provider.
